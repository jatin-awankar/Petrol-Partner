## Route consequences and geometry a buffer cannot repair

The [executable probe output](ticket10-boundary-discrepancy-probes-2026-10-04.json) confirms both absent-hole samples remain eligible up to the current schema's **1,000 m maximum**. An invented segment from [74.2833,15.80946] to [74.2853,15.80946], wholly within SOI hole 0, returns `outside:false, crossings:0` at all three bands. These are synthetic coordinates, not claimed roads or legal stops. This illustrates why missing exclusions also undermine outside-transit accounting: the existing cumulative 5,000 m / 600 s limits cannot count an excursion the boundary geometry does not represent.

Eroding the candidate around its represented edges cannot add omitted islands, create the correct internal enclave rings or adjudicate the extra component. A sufficiently large erosion might incidentally exclude some bad points while rejecting valid places; that is not a justified accuracy treatment. Missing holes require rights-cleared, positionally validated exclusion geometry or a separately approved, evidenced exclusion-region policy. Missing islands require validated source geometry or an explicit service-area restriction; a larger band does not restore them. Copying SOI coordinates or taking an SOI intersection as a deployable patch would reintroduce its unresolved reuse question and would still not prove accuracy.

## Defensible uncertainty treatment

**No numerical band is defensible from the evidence inspected.** DataMeet's source page warns of a shift without quantification. The candidate metadata, build dates and geoBoundaries topology QA supply no Maharashtra-specific error distribution, bound or validated checkpoint coverage. SOI's recorded ±12.5 m RMSE cannot be transferred to this candidate or treated as a maximum error. Dataset agreement/disagreement, scale, image pixel size and coordinate precision are not substitutes.

Once supported evidence exists, a band must account for the applicable source positional error, epoch, transformation and computation error under a documented confidence/coverage model. Statistical accuracy must be described as statistical; unknown coverage cannot be silently treated as zero uncertainty. The current requirement should not be silently reinterpreted as either an absolute mathematical guarantee or a chosen 95% success threshold. Any material change in the promised assurance needs a product decision.

Requested **and** confirmed routed endpoints must reject if outside, on a boundary, within the validated uncertainty treatment, or within any unresolved semantic region. Route crossings and near-boundary passages require the same treatment; ambiguous transit fails verification rather than receiving guessed time. Until there is a complete approved artifact and uncertainty treatment, the existing global `BOUNDARY_UNAVAILABLE` response is the only implemented fail-closed behavior. No hand-drawn mask or arbitrary buffer was installed.
