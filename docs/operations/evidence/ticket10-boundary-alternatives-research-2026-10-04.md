## Preferred evaluation candidate: geoBoundaries gbOpen / DataMeet

The [first-party API](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/) identifies `IND-ADM1-1811400`, administrative meaning “State / Union Territory”, 36 units, represented year **2011**, source update **2023-01-19**, build **2023-12-12**, and DataMeet India community / Election Commission of India provenance. A build date is not a new survey date. The advertised full archive is pinned to repository revision `9469f09`:

- [Full source archive](https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/geoBoundaries-IND-ADM1-all.zip).
- [Full GeoJSON](https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/geoBoundaries-IND-ADM1.geojson).

The API's artifact-specific licence is **CC BY 2.5 India**. The [DataMeet states page](https://projects.datameet.org/maps/states/) confirms that licence and identifies the source as scraped ECI polling-station data. It warns of positional shift, missing/incorrect names and historical state changes. It supplies no quantified Maharashtra error bound. Its example attribution incorrectly refers to parliamentary constituencies, so retain the actual dataset title and source rather than repeating that label. The [repository's generic README](https://github.com/datameet/maps) now says CC BY 4.0 unless explicitly stated; that does not erase the state-specific or pinned-release terms.

The [CC BY 2.5 IN deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/deed.en) explicitly permits commercial sharing and adaptation. [Legal code sections 3–4](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/legalcode.en-gb) permit reproduction, adaptation and distribution in different media: extraction, coordinate transformation, retained server copies and backups fit those granted acts. Retain licence/disclaimer notices, supplied author/title/source links and identify adaptations. Do not imply endorsement or restrict the original work's licence rights. This is attribution licensing, without ODbL-style derivative-database share-alike; retaining CC BY notices on the geometry does not require relicensing application code. The grant and disclaimer do not independently prove all upstream title. The public DataMeet and geoBoundaries declarations are the licence evidence available here; no separate ECI permission was established.

[geoBoundaries](https://www.geoboundaries.org/index.html) additionally publishes its database under CC BY 4.0 and requests prominent acknowledgement and a link. Keep both the source-specific terms and geoBoundaries credit. Proposed artifact notice: “Maharashtra geometry extracted from geoBoundaries IND-ADM1-1811400, sourced from DataMeet India community / Election Commission of India, India — State Boundaries, CC BY 2.5 IN. Extraction and normalization by Petrol Partner. geoBoundaries acknowledgement: Runfola et al. (2020).” Include linked source/licences and the exact processing description alongside the artifact and in a relevant product data-credit surface.

Prefer the full country file. [CGAZ documentation](https://www.geoboundaries.org/globalDownloads.html) explicitly describes extensive simplification and substituted disputed-area definitions; the global/simplified products are unsuitable substitutes for the selected full-resolution state geometry.

## Other candidates

**OpenStreetMap:** a legitimate commercial-use candidate, not automatically a suitable boundary. [ODbL 1.0 sections 3–4](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) grant commercial use, extraction, modification, temporary/permanent reproduction and distribution. Publicly used derivative databases trigger share-alike and machine-readable database/alteration access; public outputs require notices. Keeping files on a server does not by itself avoid public-use obligations. Plan an isolated, openly offered boundary derivation and preserve notices, rather than assuming all application data must be shared. [OSMF attribution guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines) requires OpenStreetMap credit and a licence link for public works, with database notices placed where recipients can find them.

OSM administrative relations are community-maintained and require complete member-way/node assembly, ring-role interpretation and topology validation; tags are not authoritative legal proof. The [community's administrative-boundary documentation](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:boundary%3Dadministrative) is a data-model source, not state accreditation. No pinned Maharashtra relation revision or quantified accuracy was validated here, so OSM is not recommended for immediate substitution. [Geofabrik](https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/india.html) offers free dated India PBF snapshots (current page: approximately 1.6 GB); pin the actual snapshot, hash it, retain replication date and all relation members, and record assembly-tool versions. Its regional `.poly` is an extraction extent, not a state boundary. Public extracts omit contributor personal metadata. No paid API is necessary, but acquisition/assembly costs exceed a prepared single-state polygon.

**GADM:** rejected for this request. Its [publisher licence](https://gadm.org/license.html) permits academic/non-commercial access but requires prior permission for commercial use or redistribution. This would recreate the pending-permission blocker.

**Natural Earth:** clear [public-domain commercial modification/distribution rights](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/), with no mandatory attribution. However, its [1:10-million Admin 1 product](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/) is cartographic, uses de facto boundary conventions, remains labelled beta and has different lake variants. Its scale and lack of suitable positional evidence do not support this route-level eligibility policy. “10m” means ten million scale, not ten-metre accuracy. Rights alone do not qualify it.

## Acquisition and processing acceptance

Do not fetch `current`, `main` or `latest` at runtime. Resolve the geoBoundaries release to its full commit, download archive plus metadata/licence, check downloaded bytes against the Git LFS object SHA-256, and record archive byte count/hash and exact command/tool versions. GitHub raw may return a small LFS pointer instead of geometry: reject that as an artifact. Extract exactly one Maharashtra feature by audited attributes; retain original properties and all rings. Hash both the original member bytes and canonical normalized geometry. The coordinator's local evaluation records actual acquired checksums; none is invented here.

Inspect the archive's CRS definition and coordinates, record longitude/latitude order and the transformation to `OGC:CRS84`; a GeoJSON suffix alone is not source-CRS verification. Validate closed rings, self/cross intersections, nesting, holes and detached islands without silent repair or simplification. Preserve tiny parts. The existing implementation's five-million-operation budget must be tested against the complete actual feature; vertex count alone does not establish bounded topology cost. Run the checker on actual border, hole and multipart locations and synthetic routes crossing its edges, then representative saved routes with real provider edge timing. Synthetic route geometry through a real polygon remains a synthetic evaluation, not proof of legal travel.

No tested source supplies a validated Maharashtra uncertainty band. Coordinate precision, polygon validity, vertex density, a 30 m snap limit, or comparison with another unvalidated map cannot establish one. A measured inter-dataset discrepancy is useful diagnostic evidence, not a maximum real-world error guarantee. A large arbitrary buffer may reduce false acceptance but cannot honestly be labelled validated source accuracy.

The coordinating agent's local acquisition verified the full GeoJSON against its LFS SHA-256 `47aa0acb6f69868daee49143276f3ce323f18905ba7d38d0a86816b405028736` (46,170,854 bytes). The selected feature is `IN-MH`, `Mahārāshtra`, shape ID `1811400B15614733245507`, with explicit CRS84 metadata; normalized geometry SHA-256 is `71ed889b914ce89fb134533c8c9f209dfbbc46f403db10744cca530906f7667d`. It contains 3 parts, no holes and 72,274 vertices, unlike SOI's previously inspected 93 parts/two holes/85,831 vertices. Shapely topology is valid and the existing checker compiles at hypothetical 10/100/1,000 m bands in approximately 20–36 ms. These local observations are preliminary evaluation, not production adoption or accuracy evidence. The large structural difference requires explicit consideration of missing/coarsened islands and holes before deciding suitability; copying SOI's parts into this artifact would recreate the unresolved reuse issue.

## Mapping-rule distinction

The [DST geospatial guidelines](https://geospatial.dst.gov.in/Guidelines.aspx) designate SOI maps/digital boundary data as the standard for political maps of India, including state boundaries. The [Indian-entity self-certification](https://geospatial.dst.gov.in/IndianEntityTermsandCondition.aspx) repeats that rule for publishing political maps. These primary sources do not establish that substituting an open polygon for internal route eligibility is either prohibited or automatically cleared. Do not present the replacement as an official political boundary or use the research to approve external operation. Existing external-review gates remain unchanged; a source licence is distinct from mapping/operating compliance. No external party was contacted.

## Recommendation and decision needed

Recommend **geoBoundaries gbOpen full-resolution DataMeet-derived Maharashtra for conditional evaluation**, with the explicit historic/shift/licence-chain limitations above. This offers the clearest low-cost prepared artifact and simpler obligations than OSM. Under the existing approved SOI-specific policy **none of these alternatives currently qualifies**: both a source amendment and an evidenced uncertainty treatment are necessary, followed by full artifact adoption and authenticated PostgreSQL checks within ticket 10.

A concrete amendment should designate this pinned dataset as an **operational service-area representation**, disclaim legal authority, preserve inside-endpoint and cumulative-transit rules, retain all audit/recovery/fail-closed requirements, and explicitly decide whether an empirical operational exclusion band can replace the current source-validated-accuracy requirement. Any chosen numeric band must come with reviewable evidence and recorded residual uncertainty. Merely approving a new source name does not satisfy that second decision. Until those decisions and checks are complete, keep production `BOUNDARY_UNAVAILABLE`, ticket 10 claimed, and real bookings disabled.
