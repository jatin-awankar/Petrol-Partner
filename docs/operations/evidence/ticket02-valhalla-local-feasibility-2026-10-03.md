# Ticket 02 — local Valhalla routing trial (2026-10-03)

**Result: technical candidate, not an approved production provider.** This was an isolated, synthetic, local run. It changed no Petrol Partner code, deployed service, or user data. It does not close ticket 02 or permit real bookings.

Valhalla's [official scripted Docker image](https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla/blob/master/docker/README.md) ran locally as version `3.9.0-53e00619f` with a [Geofabrik Western India OpenStreetMap extract](https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/india.html). The container built 928 graph tiles and answered `/status`. Test coordinates were public locations near Amravati University and Badnera, not participant journeys. No API account or payment method was used. The [Valhalla engine is MIT-licensed](https://valhalla.github.io/valhalla/); its [OpenStreetMap source data is ODbL-licensed and requires attribution](https://valhalla.github.io/valhalla/contributing/data/data-sources/). This trial does not decide the legal treatment of stored route outputs or the final display attribution.

Three `/route` requests returned HTTP 200 and one leg each. `auto` and `motorcycle` both returned 14.064 km, 1,496.320 seconds, and the same encoded geometry on this route. `motor_scooter` returned the same 14.064 km geometry with a 1,546.862-second duration and different routing cost.

The [official costing documentation](https://github.com/valhalla/valhalla-docs/blob/master/turn-by-turn/api-reference.md) describes `motor_scooter` as a motor-scooter or moped profile with a 45 km/h default top speed and `motorcycle` as a beta profile. The product owner's recorded choice remains to route Petrol Partner bikes and scooters as `motorcycle` while retaining separate vehicle categories; this trial does not silently change that policy. Identical geometry for the three modes on one route does not establish correct local two-wheeler restrictions or safe stops.

The route response included a polyline and maneuver shape indices. A second local `/trace_attributes` call using `shape_match=edge_walk` returned the same shape, 81 edges, and 182 per-shape lengths for each mode, consistent with [Valhalla's documented edge and shape attributes](https://valhalla.github.io/valhalla/api/map-matching/). On this sample, the route summary was **14.064 km**, summed edge lengths **14.062 km**, and summed per-shape lengths **13.988 km**. The 76 m difference between the summary and per-shape sum is material to integer-paise pricing. Its cause and precision must be established; do not sum the current per-shape values as the accepted distance. A proposed adapter would need one documented authoritative distance source, exact shape-position mapping, integer-metre rounding, and tests for edge boundaries, repeated geometry, endpoint snapping, and accepted-price immutability.

The local graph directory occupied about **1.7 GiB** after the build, including a 224 MiB OSM extract, a 637 MiB tile archive, an unpacked tile directory, and timezone data. Observed Docker memory was about **765 MiB during build** and **84 MiB at idle**; these are one-machine observations, not production capacity estimates. No reliable zero-cost production host was identified. [Render says its free web services sleep after inactivity, have ephemeral files, and are for testing rather than production](https://render.com/docs/free); the repository's [provider arrangement assessment](../provider-arrangement.md) likewise has no approved free real-trip topology.

**Remaining proof before selecting Valhalla:** establish OSM/Valhalla route-output retention and attribution treatment for this use; specify and validate the exact edge-to-shape distance algorithm; test local roads and restrictions for each intended category, ambiguous stops, boundaries, failures and restart behavior; measure capacity and restore from a versioned tile archive; and demonstrate an accessible, reliable ₹0 host with monitoring for real support windows. The local trial container was stopped and removed, and its temporary graph was deleted. No deployment remains.
