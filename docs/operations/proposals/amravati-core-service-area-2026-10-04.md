## Approved implementation decision

Approve an independently authored **Amravati core service area**, limited to the rectangle below, with **both endpoints and the entire saved route inside**. Outside-area travel allowance becomes **zero**. Keep the **50 km / 90-minute** trip limits and all existing eligibility, contribution, schedule, support, recovery and launch gates. Approval would authorize the documented implementation and local verification work; it would not attest that operator checks have happened, resolve ticket 10, deploy anything or enable real bookings.

The material tradeoff is explicit: the product would verify membership of its own coordinate-defined service area. It would no longer promise statewide coverage, administrative completeness or authoritative state-border verification. This replaces that requirement through a product decision; it does not claim the missing statewide evidence has passed or move it to ticket 11 or staging.

## Coverage and coordinate provenance

Proposed name/identifier: **Amravati core / `amravati-core-v1`**. Geographic limits in WGS84 longitude/latitude:

- West **77.730000° E**; east **77.830000° E**.
- South **20.890000° N**; north **20.970000° N**.
- Centre **[77.780000, 20.930000]**. Approximately **10.40 km east–west × 8.86 km north–south**, about **92.13 km²**. Distances/area are orientation estimates, not a route-length allowance.

This covers a selected central Amravati area, not all Amravati city, district, surrounding towns or Maharashtra. A place name, postal address, college affiliation or “Amravati” geocoder label does not establish eligibility. No particular campus, landmark, road or safe stop is promised to be covered. The old fixed-college corridor is not reinstated.

The four rounded limits are **newly proposed business configuration**, chosen numerically for a small local footprint. They were not traced, extracted, snapped or copied from SOI, geoBoundaries, OSM, Mapbox or a cadastral map. The [Maharashtra Gazetteer](https://gazetteers.maharashtra.gov.in/cultural.maharashtra.gov.in/english/gazetteer/AMRAVATI/places_Amravati%20Town.html) places Amravati town at 20°56′ N / 77°47′ E; that coarse historical anchor falls inside and supplies orientation only. It does not define these edges or certify their real-world accuracy. The district government's About page timed out during the coordinator's fetch, so the accessible Gazetteer is the cited anchor.

On approval, generate this exact geometry from the four limits, with no reprojection, simplification or external download. Store UTF-8 JSON in the key/order/number format below **without a trailing newline**:

## Exact operational rules

**Promise.** A successful check establishes that the recorded selections, confirmed routed endpoints and saved server-verified polyline satisfy this versioned numerical service-area rule. It does not establish legal jurisdiction, road/access rights, physical stopping safety, OSM/provider/GPS accuracy, a person's actual position, or that a driver actually stays on the route. No live tracking is added. Display: **“Amravati core service area — selected endpoints and the planned route must stay inside. Not a city or state boundary.”**

**Endpoints.** Check driver requested origin/destination and confirmed routed origin/destination separately. Keep the existing unambiguous, explicitly confirmed snap of at most 30 m; a snap cannot rescue an ineligible requested point. Passenger requested pickup/drop-off and matched route positions must also pass, retain order, the existing 30 m matching and 500 m minimum, and the existing safe-place/side/helmet declarations. The same immutable area identity/hash must be bound to preparation, later quotes, requests and acceptance. Operator approval of coverage is not approval of every stopping place inside it.

**Route exits.** The entire normalized saved polyline, including every segment between vertices, must remain in the strict eligible interior. Any exit, re-entry, touch, edge overlap or uncertain classification rejects new preparation; **no 5 km/10-minute grace** remains for this version. Do not silently reroute or clip geometry to pass. A fresh provider route requires a fresh preview/confirmation. A route with inside endpoints but an outside intermediate vertex fails. For this axis-aligned convex rectangle, checking every vertex's conservative interval is sufficient to prove containment of the straight saved segments; still test the complete route and do not reuse that shortcut for future non-convex shapes. Retain provider timing validation and the 50 km/90-minute caps; this area decision does not change distance/price normalization.

Zero permitted exit is justified because a short trip outside a roughly 10 km-wide local area could materially exceed the chosen coverage. Retaining the statewide 5 km allowance would defeat the operator's small-area scope. A 50 km route can still wind within the area, so reducing the existing length/time caps is unnecessary.

**Boundary uncertainty.** There is no estimated administrative line to calibrate: the numerical limits *define* this service boundary. Do not populate a fabricated `sourceAccuracyMetres` or describe this as zero physical-location error. Use a separate **coordinate-precision rejection rule**, tied to the existing polyline6 representation, not a 100/1,000 m geographic buffer:

For each coordinate `x` in degrees, conservatively enclose it in integer microdegrees using `[floor(1,000,000*x) - 1, ceil(1,000,000*x) + 1]`. The entire longitude interval must be strictly between **77,730,000 and 77,830,000**, and the latitude interval strictly between **20,890,000 and 20,970,000**. Do not round an outside/border value inward. For already decoded polyline6 integer coordinates use the integer itself before expanding by one unit. Validate decimal-to-integer enclosure rather than relying on floating-point equality at the thresholds.

The one-unit allowance is one polyline6 encoding quantum, **0.000001°**: it covers at most one-unit encoding quantization plus outward conversion, not road/GPS/map error. Exact boundary values and values whose envelope touches a boundary fail. A vertex exactly one microdegree inside fails; a two-microdegree-inside vertex can pass if its other axis passes. Nonfinite/out-of-range values, unsupported precision, malformed CRS, an invalid/unknown area version or inability to establish the interval fail closed. If a different coordinate encoding is introduced, its bound must be validated under a new calculation version; no silent reuse of this allowance.

Physical map/provider errors remain unknown. Consequently this proposal guarantees **coordinate-defined planned coverage only**. User location/GPS is not a verified location source; require explicit map-point confirmation. If the operator cannot identify a proposed stop on the ground or suspects a map mismatch near an edge, reject that stop and choose a clearly understood interior place; do not widen the polygon or claim the numerical allowance solves the ambiguity. If actual-ground containment rather than saved-coordinate containment is required, this proposal is insufficient and needs a separate evidence-based requirement.

**Failure, updates and history.** Only a pinned locally packaged artifact and recorded operator approval may enable the area verifier. No client geometry, environment “approved” flag, downloaded latest artifact or retained test fixture can enable it. Missing/mismatched artifact or approval fails closed. Store area ID/version, geometry hash, authoring provenance, rule/calculation version and approval reference in the existing route snapshot, audit-linked operation and independent recovery receipt. Preserve historical formats/terms. Successful retries return original results even after area unavailability. Do not silently recalculate old routes after an area change. Existing cancellation/hold/incident policy governs affected future commitments; active-trip visibility remains available. Any changed corner, added hole or new component is a fresh scope/version decision, not a routine data refresh.
