# Driver and car review evidence

Driver licence, car registration and insurance, applicable car documents, and permission-to-use evidence use the same restricted private-storage adapter and 512 KB JPEG/PNG/PDF validation as student evidence. The application stores object keys, byte counts, digests, status, reviewer decisions, and dates in PostgreSQL; document bytes remain in private storage. Operator access is one-use, valid for five minutes, and audited. A decision schedules raw evidence deletion after six days. Rejected applicants can replace documents; the prior object enters a separate durable deletion queue. The worker retries failed deletion, and `/v1/verification/admin/driver-car-evidence-retention` reports overdue and failed records from both queues.

Production intake stays closed unless the private provider is verified and `PILOT_DRIVER_CAR_REVIEW_RECEIPT_RETENTION_VERIFIED=true`. The latter flag must only be set after the lifecycle of independent driver-car review receipts and their personal-data snapshots is evidenced. These receipts are required for recovery after an older database restore. The synthetic integration tests do not establish provider-side erasure, backup expiry, or a real receipt retention horizon. The separate pilot launch gates in `docs/pilot-spec.md` still apply.

The review API requires an individual allowlisted operator account with MFA and an `Idempotency-Key`. Driver, car, and driver-car association decisions are independent. A decision commits its state, audit record, and applicant notification work in PostgreSQL; success is returned only after its signed independent receipt is durable. A pending receipt keeps protected writes restricted until reconciliation. The operator recovery flow replays receipts and restores missing decision, subject, and evidence-retention records before writes may reopen. Eligibility checks read current PostgreSQL state and expiry dates in the offer and booking paths, independent of the worker.

Applicant API: `PUT /v1/verification/driver-eligibility`, `POST /v1/verification/vehicles`, `PUT /v1/verification/vehicles/:id/pilot-documents`, `POST /v1/verification/driver-vehicle-associations`, and `POST /v1/verification/driver-car-evidence/:subjectType/:subjectId/:purpose`. Applicants can read their own current statuses through `/v1/verification/overview`. Operators use `/v1/verification/admin/pending`, the evidence-access endpoints, and `POST /v1/verification/admin/driver-car/:subjectType/:subjectId/review`.
