## Cancellation, incidents, and response

- A passenger may cancel before departure; the protected action releases the allocation and records actor, time, and optional reason. Driver cancellation explicitly cancels the offer and all affected bookings and queues notices. There is no automatic cancellation or no-show charge. A material route, time, vehicle, capacity, stop, or price change after a request requires audited cancellation and replacement under ticket 02, not a silent transfer. After departure, absence, interruption, attempted cancellation, safety concern, or disagreement enters incident review; no automatic journey outcome or contribution is created.
- Participants facing imminent danger should contact local emergency services first, then report through the published support channel when available. The on-duty operator may immediately pause new bookings and place holds. Safety revocation blocks departure; an active-trip safety report creates a high-priority incident with preserved trip visibility and an auditable decision. Operator access and action must follow the existing MFA, authorization, and audit controls.
- On 2026-10-03 Jatin Awankar committed to personal acknowledgement of urgent active-trip or safety reports within 15 minutes throughout coverage, including after 18:00 until active trips are resolved, and a first substantive routine response within one business day. Rehearse before publication or launch. These are response targets, not resolution guarantees; an automated delivery acknowledgement does not count as a personal response. For routine cases, one business day means nine covered hours accumulated Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 Asia/Kolkata, starting on receipt during coverage or at the next opening otherwise. Urgent reports about active trips start the clock immediately, including extended coverage.
- Record receipt, priority, human acknowledgement, owner, next update time and outcome. A credible safety concern is urgent even if the reporter cannot provide a booking ID. At acknowledgement the operator sets a case-specific update time; a missed update escalates, without automatically deciding travel, debt or receipt. A missed 15-minute urgent acknowledgement, unavailable operator or unavailable support channel pauses new commitments and prevents unsupported departures. Preserve active-trip visibility and record the failed-contact incident for reconciliation. In synthetic testing, exercise an explicitly simulated escalation path; before real trips, establish and rehearse the independent escalation path. Pause alone cannot resolve an active incident. A willing backup, independent channel and missed-contact rehearsal remain unestablished. Emergency services are the first route for imminent danger, not a substitute for accountable platform case handling.
