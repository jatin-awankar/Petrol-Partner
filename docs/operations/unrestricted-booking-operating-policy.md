## Area, schedule, and commitments

- Jatin Awankar selected Maharashtra as the first-release service area on 2026-09-29, replacing the proposed Amravati-area limit. Both pickup and drop-off must be inside the state; brief travel outside may occur only along the server-verified posted route. The exact authoritative boundary dataset, version, and server-side enforcement remain to be approved before activation. This state-level launch restriction changes ticket 02's earlier choice of no geographic restriction; update that record as part of the coordinated approval before implementation relies on either rule.
- The [Survey of India administrative-boundary database](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/administrative-boundary-data-base-abdb-) is an official candidate for the versioned state geometry. The [online product listing](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/Digital_Products.aspx) lists an administrative-boundary shapefile at ₹0. No file, edition, checksum, coordinate system, or production-reuse permission has yet been verified; the [published copyright policy](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/copyright-policy) must be reconciled before bundling or serving its data. Do not approximate the state with a bounding box or accept a geocoder's state label as the sole server-side boundary check.
- A posted ride may cover at most 50 km and 90 minutes of server-verified routed travel. The 30-minute overlap buffer follows expected arrival and is separate from the 90-minute trip cap. The driver may post from 2 hours to 7 days before departure.
- Departures may be scheduled Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST. No cross-timezone trip is supported at launch. The primary operator must monitor the published support channel during the entire operating window and remain available until every active trip has an explicit outcome or documented handoff. Pause new bookings whenever primary coverage is unavailable. An on-duty operator may act outside published hours for an active-trip incident.
- Requests close 60 minutes before scheduled departure. The driver must accept or reject by 30 minutes before departure. Unanswered pending requests expire at that cutoff and never become confirmed bookings. Acceptance rechecks current declarations, restrictions, holds, route and vehicle terms, seat capacity, overlap, and support coverage in the protected server transaction. One accepted passenger occupies one seat for the whole ride; no segment reuse.
- Departure is permitted from 15 minutes before through 30 minutes after the scheduled time, subject to server checks of current declarations, holds, route/vehicle and confirmed passenger state, overlap, and support coverage. The driver records who boarded. An unstarted ride after +30 minutes is delayed and needs explicit driver or operator resolution. Time alone never starts or cancels it.

## Cancellation, incidents, and response

- A passenger may cancel before departure; the protected action releases the allocation and records actor, time, and optional reason. Driver cancellation explicitly cancels the offer and all affected bookings and queues notices. There is no automatic cancellation or no-show charge. A material route, time, vehicle, capacity, stop, or price change after a request requires audited cancellation and replacement under ticket 02, not a silent transfer. After departure, absence, interruption, attempted cancellation, safety concern, or disagreement enters incident review; no automatic journey outcome or contribution is created.
- Participants facing imminent danger should contact local emergency services first, then report through the published support channel when available. The on-duty operator may immediately pause new bookings and place holds. Safety revocation blocks departure; an active-trip safety report creates a high-priority incident with preserved trip visibility and an auditable decision. Operator access and action must follow the existing MFA, authorization, and audit controls.
- The proposed targets are acknowledgement of urgent active-trip or safety cases within 15 minutes during coverage and response to routine booking or settlement cases within one business day. These are **not published promises**. Jatin Awankar must rehearse receipt and acknowledgement using the final contact and escalation path and approve achievable targets before publication. A failed urgent contact pauses new bookings and triggers a separately arranged escalation channel. No such channel or backup is yet established; active-trip escalation is not ready.

## Retention and history

The provisional maximum for unused onboarding records is 30 days. Routine unrestricted booking history and direct contact data are to be deleted or anonymized within 90 days after account closure or resolution of the last trip or case, whichever is later. An incident or legal hold needs a recorded reason, owner, scope, and review date; review it every 30 days and record continuation or release. Do not erase an active commitment or unresolved case. The detailed schedule for declarations, operational receipts, provider copies, backups, exports, legacy platform-payment records, and restoration-safe deletion remains to be approved after the deployed-data and provider inventory. The existing seven-day raw-evidence rule belongs to the historical document-review flow and is not a reason to collect new documents for self-declared unrestricted eligibility. No live deletion is authorized by this proposed policy.

The [account-closure inventory](account-closure-retention.md) currently queues closure and hold decisions but has no provider deletion runner or independently held deletion manifest. The [backup procedure](database-backup-recovery.md) also requires a finite, tested lifecycle for encrypted database copies and independent receipts. Before approving a detailed schedule, inventory each active and historical data class, its provider copies and backups, the minimum recovery horizon, legal or incident holds, and the exact deletion or anonymization operation. Rehearse an older-snapshot restore after deletion and prove that deleted personal information is not silently resurrected. Until then, closure requests must not be described as completed erasure.

The PRMITR fixed corridor, its six proposed stop pairs and landmarks, verified-student and approved-vehicle labels, weekday 09:00–18:00 support proposal, 10:00–10:30/15:30/17:30 departure observations, provisional 20-minute journey plus 15-minute buffer, and whole-vehicle ₹5/₹7 contribution caps remain [historical corridor-development rules](../pilot-operating-policy.md). The currently implemented car-only University–PRMITR contract and any records created under it retain their original policy versions, eligibility decisions, route/stop terms, contributions, and payment history. The old 60/30-minute deadlines and −15/+30-minute departure window are adopted independently above for the new policy; their appearance in old records does not migrate those records to this version. Do not reprice or reinterpret historical records.
